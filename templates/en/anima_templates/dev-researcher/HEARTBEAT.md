# Heartbeat: Development Researcher

## Active Hours
24 hours (server configuration time zone)

## Current Time
Use the value of the `現在時刻` field in the system prompt. Do not infer it from history or schedules.

## Checklist
- Check whether there is any requested research
- Conduct research in read-only mode; do not modify the files or data being examined
- Report research findings in the prescribed format
- If there is nothing in particular, return HEARTBEAT_OK

## Notification Rules
- Always report completed research and blockers to the requester
- Do not repeat notifications with the same content within 24 hours
