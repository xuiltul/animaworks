Read the originals before deciding; similarity is not evidence of duplication. Preserve entity-specific detail, provenance, `[IMPORTANT]` rules, and the original records. If uncertain, leave them unchanged and report a proposal. Do not rewrite IDENTITY.md, SOUL.md, AGENTS.md, permissions, or raw episodes, and do not scan or compress the whole memory library.

Only confirmed duplicate knowledge or obsolete procedures may be updated or archived; name the surviving source and reason. Do not create abstract concepts merely to meet a quota. Do not delegate tasks or send messages during maintenance.
