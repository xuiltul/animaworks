Notes on where to record things:
- Don’t record unsupported negative claims such as “X can’t be used” or “you must not do X” in knowledge/・skills/.
- Record temporary, environment-dependent failures or unresolved trial and error in episodes/, not knowledge/.
- Record cases tied to PR numbers, SHAs, or multiple dates in episodes/.
